# No-go map (I) — the emergent-Lorentz naturalness problem, and why it makes holography *doubly* essential for SSV

**Status: a real NEGATIVE for standalone SSV, with a grounded holographic escape.**
The emergent-Lorentz picture SSV rests on faces a famous, established obstruction —
the **naturalness / radiative-stability problem of Lorentz invariance** (Collins,
Perez, Sudarsky, Urrutia & Vucetich 2004). As a **bosonic, non-supersymmetric**
superfluid, SSV **lacks** the two robust known protections (fermionic Fermi-point
topology; SUSY), and the weak-coupling mechanism (IR fixed point) is too weak. So
**standalone SSV fails this no-go — its emergent Lorentz is fine-tuned, not
natural.** But the *one* mechanism that works for a bosonic theory is **strong /
holographic dynamics** (Bednik, Pujolàs & Sibiryakov 2013), which power-law
suppresses Lorentz violation. That is the **same holographic container already
shown structurally essential for SSV's gravity (#166)** — so holography is
essential *twice over*, for two independent reasons.

Pre-registered on [#172](https://github.com/StigNorland/SVT/issues/172) before the
literature was pulled. This note is grounded in **real sources** (cited below);
throughout I separate *what the sources established* from *our SSV-specific
inference*.

## The obstruction (established)

**Collins–Perez–Sudarsky–Urrutia–Vucetich, PRL 93, 191301 (2004)**
[arXiv:gr-qc/0403053]. Combining known particle interactions with a **Planck-scale
preferred frame** produces, through radiative corrections, **Lorentz violation at
the percent level — ~20 orders of magnitude larger** than the naive
`(E/E_Planck)^n` estimate — *unless the bare parameters are unnaturally
fine-tuned*. The mechanism: integrating out high-momentum modes generates
Lorentz-violating operators of dimension ≤ 4, so **different fields acquire
different "speeds of light,"** and the mismatch survives to low energy. Percent-
level LV is grossly excluded by experiment, so any emergent-Lorentz theory needs a
*mechanism* that forbids the dimension-≤4 LV operators.

**Why it targets SSV directly.** SSV *has* a Planck-scale preferred frame — the
condensate rest frame, active in the `z=2` UV (below the healing length ξ). Its own
literature already accepts LV signatures: deformed dispersion relations, an
effective photon mass, and a **vacuum Cherenkov effect** (Zloshchastiev,
arXiv:2011.11897, arXiv:1003.0657). The naturalness problem says those deviations
are *not* naturally small.

## The known protections — and which SSV has

| mechanism | how it protects | fermionic/bosonic | does SSV have it? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fermi-point topology** (Volovik) | momentum-space topological charge `N=±1` pins a shared linear (Weyl) dispersion, insensitive to trans-Planckian detail | **fermionic** | **No** — SSV is a *bosonic* condensate (log-GPE), no Fermi point |
| **SUSY custodial** (Nibbelink–Pospelov) | SUSY forbids dim-≤4 LV operators → LV pushed to dim-5 (UV-suppressed) | needs SUSY | **No** — SSV is not supersymmetric (and the mechanism is itself caveated: Lorentz ⊂ SUSY algebra, and SUSY must break at low E) |
| **IR fixed point** (Chadha–Nielsen 1983) | RG flow drives sector velocities toward a common `c` in the IR | generic, weak coupling | **Partial but too weak** — convergence is only **logarithmic**, cannot reach the observed precision from a Planck UV |
| **Strong / holographic dynamics** (Bednik–Pujolàs–Sibiryakov 2013) | strong-coupling RG flow suppresses velocity spread **power-law** in IR/UV; light bound states lock to a common `c` | generic, needs strong dynamics | **Only via the container** — requires an SSV holographic dual |

**The Goldstone point (the crux for a bosonic theory).** SSV's naturally protected
object is the **phonon** — the Goldstone mode of the spontaneously broken `U(1)`.
But Goldstone's theorem protects the phonon's **gaplessness**, *not* a universal
speed: a Goldstone propagates at `c` **iff the ground state is Lorentz invariant**,
and a superfluid ground state is *not* (it has a rest frame). So the bosonic
protection covers *one* mode's masslessness, **not** the equality of speeds across
sectors that Lorentz invariance requires — exactly the quantity the naturalness
problem attacks (Goldstone-gravity literature: PRD 80, 025020; hep-th/0203221).

## Verdict (against the pre-registered rule)

- **Standalone SSV: FAILS the no-go (real negative).** A bosonic, non-SUSY
  superfluid has neither Fermi-point topology nor SUSY, and the weak-coupling IR
  fixed point converges only logarithmically. Its emergent Lorentz invariance is
  therefore **radiatively fine-tuned, not natural.** This is consistent with — and
  independently reinforces — the standing result that **SSV is not viable as a
  standalone theory.** It is a proof-carrying negative, not a softenable one.
- **SSV-in-a-container: a grounded escape.** The *only* protection available to a
  bosonic theory is **strong / holographic dynamics** (Bednik–Pujolàs–Sibiryakov),
  which power-law-suppresses LV — natural. This is the **same container already
  shown essential for SSV's gravity (#166)**. So the container is **doubly
  essential**: it supplies the transverse-traceless gravity a superfluid cannot
  shear into being, *and* the Lorentz-naturalness protection a bosonic superfluid
  cannot generate itself.

**Net:** the two deepest problems of a standalone superfluid vacuum — *no
transverse gravity* and *radiatively-unstable Lorentz invariance* — have the **same
cure**, the holographic container. The programme's central thesis (holography is
structural, not a patch) now has a **second, independent, literature-grounded
pillar.**

## Honesty items and scope (rule 1)

- **The escape is a positive result → suggestive, not proof.** Bednik–Pujolàs–
  Sibiryakov show strong/holographic dynamics *can* give power-law Lorentz
  emergence *in a subclass of models* — an existence proof, not a demonstration
  that *SSV's specific* dual does it. Whether SSV's holographic container achieves
  the power-law suppression (light bound-state velocities locked to a common `c`)
  is **open**.
- **The negative is the firm part.** That *standalone* bosonic SSV lacks the robust
  protections is the well-grounded conclusion; it strengthens the closure, it does
  not reopen SSV-alone.
- **Connection to our own flow (#170).** Our `z=2→z=1` emergent-Lorentz flow was the
  *weak-coupling, single-mode* picture (Chadha–Nielsen-like). The naturalness
  problem is about **multiple sectors with different speeds** converging, and
  requires the *power-law* (strong/holographic) rate. So #170 showed the flow
  *exists*; this note shows it must be **strong** (container-driven) to be natural.
- **Attribution hedges.** "Reentrant violation of special relativity in the
  low-energy corner" (hep-ph/0101286) I attribute to Klinkhamer–Volovik from
  memory; the Goldstone "speed = c iff LI ground state" statement is paraphrased
  from the Goldstone-gravity literature, not a direct quote.

## The computable follow-up (next issue)

Turn the crux into an SSV-specific computation: take **two** bosonic fields with
*different* UV velocities coupled to the condensate, evolve the RG/scale flow of
their velocity **difference**, and measure whether it closes **power-law** (natural
— container-strength) or only **logarithmic** (fine-tuned — standalone-strength).
That directly tests whether SSV's dynamics is "strong enough," and connects to the
#170 flow machinery.
