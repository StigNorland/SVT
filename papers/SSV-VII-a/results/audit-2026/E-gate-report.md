# SSV-VII-a — E-gate report

Gate: **E-GATE FAIL** · #189

## E1 — the Gausson does not exist under the adopted branch

`eq:gausson` is a solution only of the attractive branch ($b>0$ in
Zloshchastiev's convention), which the D1 decision rejected because it makes the
uniform vacuum modulationally unstable and cannot support $c_s=c$.

**§"Saturation by the Gausson" has no solution to stand on.**

## E2 — the $\hbar/2$ derivation is circular, independently of E1

The section claims the prefactor is *"derived directly from the LogSE itself,
without importing it from the standard wave-packet calculation"*, offering as
evidence that the result is *"independent of the Gausson width $\sigma$ and
therefore independent of the LogSE coupling $b$"*.

That independence is the tell. **Every** Gaussian saturates
$\Delta x\,\Delta p=\hbar/2$; it is a property of Gaussians, not of the LogSE.
The computation actually performed — Gaussian moments giving $\sigma^2/2$ and
$\hbar^2/2\sigma^2$, then multiplying — **is** the standard wave-packet
calculation. The LogSE contributed only the claim that its stationary state is
Gaussian, and under E1 it has no such state.

**Verdict `MISDERIVED`.** The $\hbar/2$ result is imported, not derived. This
holds on either branch.

## Consequence

The section must be withdrawn or rewritten from a different starting point. The
uncertainty principle may still be recoverable as "hydraulic stiffness of the
vacuum" (the paper's framing), but not by this route.
