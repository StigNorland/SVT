## The paper is honest about its own status

It states plainly that the Paper-II chiral-shear photon at $c_\perp$
*"cannot be the final physical interpretation because the observed photon
propagates at $c$"*, proposes the Goldstone phase mode as the repair, and closes
with *"The present paper formulates that problem."* It does not claim to have
solved it. That framing is correct and rule-1 compliant.

## But the proposed repair has since been closed negatively

**#180 P4 (S route) FAIL:** *one scalar Goldstone does not furnish two
transverse photon helicities.* A single phase mode of a one-component order
parameter is one degree of freedom; the photon needs two. The Goldstone-photon
identification therefore cannot work as stated, independently of its propagation
speed.

SSV-Goldstone predates that finding, so this is not an error in the paper — but
the paper's central proposal is now known to fail, and the draft must record it.

**Action:** add the #180 P4 result. The open problem the paper formulates is
resolved, and resolved negatively.
