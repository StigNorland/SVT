Generated by `instruments/tools/fetch_cited.py` — do not edit by hand.

Primary sources retrieved to verify load-bearing citations. **The files
themselves are not tracked**: this repository is public and arXiv's
distribution licence does not generally permit third-party redistribution.
Everything needed to reproduce the audit is here — fetch the identifier,
verify the SHA-256, and you hold the identical file that was read.

Local (gitignored) layout: `pdf/<key>.pdf`, `txt/<key>.txt`.
Canonical bibliographic entries for the complete series are in
`references.bib`; BibTeX selects the subset cited by each paper.
Identifiers, access states, paragraph requirements and verification
statuses are canonical in `verification.json`. Claim-specific
quotations and use assessments are tracked in
`notes/<key>.md`; accessible transcriptions of image-only source pages
are tracked separately in `transcripts/<key>.md`.

Re-fetch with `python instruments/tools/fetch_cited.py`;
check bytes without touching the network with `--verify`.

## Evidence rule

**Paragraph evidence is required by default.** Every row below links
to `notes/<key>.md`, which identifies the SSV claim, reproduces the
complete source paragraph, gives a page/section locator, and assesses
whether the source is used correctly. A paragraph-free record passes
only when `verification.json` contains a validated, reasoned exception
(an inaccessible primary or a reproducible full-source absence search).
A reader can therefore check any verdict in this audit
without re-downloading anything — which is precisely what was missing when
the faults recorded in #183 were introduced.
