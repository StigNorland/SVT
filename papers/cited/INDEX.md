## Evidence rule

**No C-gate verdict without a verbatim quotation.** Every row below links
to `notes/<key>.md`, which reproduces the actual paragraph or equation the
verdict rests on. A reader can therefore check any verdict in this audit
without re-downloading anything — which is precisely what was missing when
the faults recorded in #183 were introduced.
