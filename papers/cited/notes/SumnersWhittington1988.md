## Accessible primary abstract

The abstract reports:

> Using Kesten's pattern theorem, it is shown that almost all sufficiently
> long self-avoiding walks on the three-dimensional simple cubic lattice
> contain a knot

The theorem gives exponential rarity of the unknotted class and therefore
supports “exponentially generic.” It does not by itself derive SSV's later
fixed-knot exponent shift \(\theta\to\theta+N_K\).
