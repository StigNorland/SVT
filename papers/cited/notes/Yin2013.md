## Source result and explanation

The abstract, printed p. 1, reports:

> Here, we strictly closed the loopholes by observing a 12 h continuous
> violation of the Bell inequality and concluded that the lower bound speed
> of spooky action was 4 orders of magnitude of the speed of light if Earth's
> speed in any inertial reference frame was less than \(10^{-3}\) time the
> speed of light.

Equation (7), printed p. 4, gives `V_sa/c ≥ 1.37×10⁴` for the specified
preferred-frame speed. The conclusion explicitly notes that such a fast
hypothetical action could explain bipartite correlations without enabling
superluminal communication.

**Verdict: `OK`.** The citation supports a conditional lower bound on a
hypothetical nonlocal influence, not detection of a usable signal. SSV's
distinction between that model and a global-state account is therefore
consistent with the source's stated scope.
