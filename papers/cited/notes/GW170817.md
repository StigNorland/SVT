## SSV usage

SSV-II `main.tex:2637–2652` invokes GW170817 for simultaneous gravitational
and electromagnetic propagation, quotes a roughly 1.7-second separation, and
assigns that separation entirely to source physics. Later summaries and
SSV-Alpha use the event as a propagation-speed constraint.

## Source result and explanation

The abstract, p. 1, reports the directly observed timing fact:

> The Fermi Gamma-ray Burst Monitor independently detected a gamma-ray burst
> (GRB 170817A) with a time delay of ~1.7 s with respect to the merger time.

Section 2.1, p. 4, gives `T_0-t_c=1.734±0.054 s`. This broad multi-messenger
paper establishes association and timing, but it does not decompose that
delay into an intrinsic emission lag and a propagation lag, nor does it derive
a bound on `(c_{\rm gw}-c)/c`. Those require the dedicated fundamental-physics
analysis and an assumption or range for the intrinsic emission delay.

**Verdict: `MISREAD`.** The observed 1.7-second separation is cited correctly.
The stronger statements that the delay is *entirely* source physics and that
this paper itself proves equal propagation speeds are not supported by this
source. SSV should cite the dedicated speed-bound paper and state its
intrinsic-lag assumption.
