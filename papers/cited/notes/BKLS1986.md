## Primary result

The abstract states:

> By evaluating this contribution in a simplified case, we show that in
> general it can be expected to be finite and proportional to the area

The paper traces over field degrees of freedom inside a surface, obtains the
exterior Gaussian density matrix, and calculates its entropy. Its continuum
discussion gives entropy per unit area proportional to the inverse-square
position cutoff.

**Verdict: `OK`.** This is a direct foundational source for the regulated
cross-boundary entanglement area law used by SSV. It establishes the
mechanism and scaling, not SSV's model-specific coefficient.
