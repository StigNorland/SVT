## Reproduced passage and account

Fernandes reproduces Reichenbach's statistical characterization from
pp. 54–55:

> The direction of physical processes, and with it the direction of time, is
> thus explained as a statistical trend: the act of becoming

The sentence continues by identifying becoming with transition from
improbable to probable molecular configurations. Fernandes' reconstruction
also shows that Reichenbach appeals to the global entropy slope and the
orientation of branch systems. His account is Boltzmannian, but it is not
identical to Albert's modern Past Hypothesis: Reichenbach allows the entropy
increase to characterize our temporal region without requiring that it begin
at an absolutely initial low-entropy boundary.

**Verdict: `OK` with attribution scope.** Reichenbach supports the statistical
entropic direction used by SSV. He should not by himself be cited for the
stronger, specifically initial-boundary version of the Past Hypothesis. The
closed primary is recorded explicitly; the quotation and its page locator
come through the open scholarly reconstruction rather than an unverified
snippet.
