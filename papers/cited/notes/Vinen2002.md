## Accessible primary evidence and open proxy

The authors' institutional abstract says:

> The paper presents an extended review of our knowledge and understanding
> of turbulence in a superfluid (\(^4\)He and \(^3\)He-B), a system in which
> turbulent flow can be greatly influenced by quantum effects such as those
> leading to the restriction of rotational superfluid flow to quantized
> vortex lines.

That supports the general subject only. For transparent checking of the
detailed uses, the pinned open review Barenghi2014 (local PDF sha256
`1d2967503ac87030`) supplies the following proxy evidence:

> The second property comprises Kelvin waves, which are helical displacements
> of the vortex core which rotate with angular velocity
> \(\omega\sim\kappa k^2\), where \(k\) is the one-dimensional wavenumber
> (shorter waves rotate faster).

On its printed p. 5 it also states that Vinen/ultra-quantum tangles decay as
\(\mathcal L\sim t^{-1}\). Since
\(\kappa=h/m=2\pi\hbar/m\), the standard local-induction coefficient
\(\kappa/(4\pi)\) equals \(\hbar/(2m)\), consistent with SSV's leading
Kelvin dispersion; the core-dependent logarithm and order-unity constant
need the inaccessible detailed derivation.

**Verdict: `PENDING-PRIMARY`.** The open proxy supports the Kelvin-wave
\(k^2\) scaling and Vinen-regime \(t^{-1}\) decay, and the latter is
equivalent to a quadratic decay equation under constant coefficients.
However, the cited 2002 review itself has not been checked locally for the
exact logarithmic dispersion or normalization. SSV's further identification
\(\beta=c_{\rm rec}\) and its claimed exact entropy-production equality are
not results of the cited review and require an independent derivation.
