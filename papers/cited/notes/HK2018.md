## SSV usage

SSV-II `main.tex:2116–2127` identifies a future precision determination of the
PMNS Dirac CP phase by DUNE or Hyper-Kamiokande as the measurement needed to
test an eventual SSV relation to the CKM phase.

## Source result and explanation

Section I.1.A, p. 23, gives the design exposure and projected phase precision:

> it is expected that the leptonic CP phase δCP can be determined to better
> than 23 degrees for all possible values of δCP

The same paragraph specifies `1.3 MW × 10^8 s` integrated beam power and
projects CP-violation discovery above `3σ` (`5σ`) for 76% (57%) of the phase
space. Section III.1.A.6, p. 217, refines the phase uncertainty to `7.2°` at
`δCP=0°` or `180°`, and `23°` at `δCP=±90°`.

**Verdict: `OK`.** The report directly supports SSV's narrow statement that
Hyper-Kamiokande can produce a precision determination of the leptonic Dirac
CP phase. It does not support SSV's proposed relation between PMNS and CKM
phases; SSV correctly labels that relation as awaiting its own calculation.
