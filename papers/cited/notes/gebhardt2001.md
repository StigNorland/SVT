## SSV usage

SSV-VI `main.tex:123–130` and `main.tex:873–878` uses M33's prominent spiral

of the retired claim that a central black hole drives spiral arms.

## Source evidence

The abstract reports the dynamical-model result:

> Three-integral dynamical models fit Hubble Space Telescope WFPC2 photometry
> and STIS spectroscopy best if the central black hole mass is zero.

Section 5 gives the best-fit mass as zero for each tested light profile and
adopts a conservative upper limit

```tex
M_\bullet < 1500\,M_\odot.
```

The conclusion repeats that the HST limit is `1500 M⊙`, far below the mass
expected from the bulge correlation.

**Verdict: `OK`.** SSV's “essentially no central black hole” is a fair summary
of a zero best fit with a 1,500-solar-mass upper limit. The observational source
supports the premise used by SSV's separate mechanism-level falsifier.
