## SSV usage

SSV-III `main.tex:1348–1364` invokes Loschmidt's objection for the existence
of a formally reversed trajectory under microreversible dynamics. SSV accepts
that mathematical inverse but argues that realizing it requires preparation
of the corresponding incoming boundary data.

## Primary result and transcription

The primary scan is image-only. Printed p. 139 gives the relevant sentence:

> Offenbar muss ganz allgemein, in jedem beliebigen System, der gesamte
> Verlauf der Begebenheiten rückläufig werden, wenn momentan die
> Geschwindigkeiten aller seiner Elemente umgekehrt werden.

In English: in any system, the entire course of events must run backward if
the velocities of all its elements are instantaneously reversed. Loschmidt's
following sentence presents simultaneous reversal of all atomic velocities
as the simple prescription for undoing what occurred.

**Verdict: `OK`.** This is direct support for SSV's description of the
Loschmidt reversal: velocity-reversible microscopic dynamics admit the
formally reversed history. The cited work does not establish SSV's further
claim that a physical medium will fail to prepare the required global
boundary data; that is explicitly SSV's answer to the objection.
