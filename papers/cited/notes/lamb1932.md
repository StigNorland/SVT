### p.241 — Art. 163, circular-section passage

$$\psi = -\frac{\omega}{2\pi}\,\varpi_0 \iint\left(\log\frac{8\varpi_0}{r_1} - 2\right)dx'\,d\varpi'$$

The constant **−2** here belongs to the *stream function* $\psi$, an
intermediate quantity — **not** to the energy.

### p.239 — Art. 162, "The energy of any system of circular vortices"

$$T = \pi\rho\iint (u^2+v^2)\,\varpi\,dx\,d\varpi
    = \pi\rho\iint\left(v\frac{\partial\psi}{\partial x} - u\frac{\partial\psi}{\partial x}\right)dx\,d\varpi
    = -\pi\rho\iint \psi\omega\,dx\,d\varpi = -\pi\rho\,\Sigma\kappa\psi \tag{1}$$

> by a partial integration, the integrated terms vanishing at the limits. We
> have here used $\kappa$ to denote the strength $\omega\,\delta x\,\delta\varpi$
> of an elementary vortex-filament.

Also on p.239: impulse $P=\pi\rho\,\Sigma\kappa\varpi^2$ (3), and the
'circular axis' definitions $x_0$, $\varpi_0^2$ (4).

**Art. 162 gives only the *general* energy expression $T=-\pi\rho\Sigma\kappa\psi$.
It contains no logarithm and no explicit ring formula.**

### p.240 — Art. 163 opens

> **163.** Let us now consider, in particular, the case of an isolated
> vortex-ring the dimensions of whose cross-section are small compared with the
> radius $(\varpi_0)$. It has been shown that

$$\psi = -\frac1\pi\iint\left\{F_1\!\left(\frac{r_2-r_1}{r_2+r_1}\right)-E_1\!\left(\frac{r_2-r_1}{r_2+r_1}\right)\right\}(r_1+r_2)\,\omega'\,dx'\,d\varpi' \tag{1}$$

## Correction to an earlier E-gate suggestion

The E-gate note initially flagged that SSV-I cites §163 for the self-energy
"but the energy appears to be §162", based on the OCR. **That suggestion is
withdrawn.** The supplied pages show Art. 162 states only the general
$T=-\pi\rho\Sigma\kappa\psi$; it is Art. **163** that specialises to an isolated
thin-cored ring and derives the explicit results. **SSV-I's citation of §163 is
correct.**

### p.240 — Art. 163 continued, the thin-core limit

> where $r_1$, $r_2$ are defined by Art. 161 (6). For points $(x,\varpi)$ in or
> near the substance of the vortex, the ratio $r_1/r_2$ is small, and the
> modulus $(\lambda)$ of the elliptic integrals is accordingly nearly equal to
> unity. We then have

$$F_1(\lambda)=\tfrac12\log\frac{16}{\lambda'^2},\qquad E_1(\lambda)=1 \tag{2}$$

$$\lambda'^2 = 1-\lambda^2 = \frac{4r_1r_2}{(r_1+r_2)^2} \tag{3}$$

> or $\lambda'^2 = 4r_1/r_2$, nearly.

(The scan renders this last as "$5r_1/r_2$"; eq. (3) with $r_1\ll r_2$ gives
$4r_1/r_2$, so the 5 is a print/scan artifact. Immaterial here.)

> Hence at points within the substance of the vortex the value of $\psi$ is of
> the order $\kappa\varpi_0\log(\varpi_0/\epsilon)$ … We can now estimate the
> magnitude of the velocity $dx_0/dt$ of translation of the vortex-ring. By Art.
> 162 (1), $T$ is of the order $\rho\kappa^2\varpi_0\log(\varpi_0/\epsilon)$ …

Confirms Art. 162 (1) is used only as an *order-of-magnitude* input here; the
explicit results come later in Art. 163.

### p.241 — Art. 163, running head "162–163] Speed of a Vortex-Ring"

> **For the case of a *circular* section more definite results can be obtained
> as follows. If we neglect the variations of $\varpi$ and $\omega$ over the
> section**, the formulae (1) and (2) give

$$\psi=-\frac{\omega}{2\pi}\varpi_0\iint\left(\log\frac{8\varpi_0}{r_1}-2\right)dx'\,d\varpi'$$

$$\psi=-\frac{\omega}{2\pi}\varpi_0\int_0^a\!\!\int_0^{2\pi}\left(\log\frac{8\varpi_0}{r_1}-2\right)s'\,ds'\,d\chi' \tag{4}$$

> where $a$ is the radius of the section.

## DECISIVE — the core model

$\omega$ is the **vorticity**. "Neglect the variations of $\varpi$ and $\omega$
over the section" is exactly the statement that **the vorticity is uniform over
a circular core of radius $a$**.

That is the uniform-vorticity core, which is the model yielding the
$\log(8\varpi_0/a)-\tfrac74$ energy constant (and $-\tfrac14$ for the
translation velocity).

**Consequence for SSV-I:** `eq:Ekin`'s $-\tfrac74$ is the *correct* Lamb result
and is *correctly* cited to §163. The fault is in the **appendix**, whose
Neumann self-inductance formula
$\left[(2/\varepsilon-\varepsilon)K-(2/\varepsilon)E\right]$ describes a
*filament/hollow* core and yields $-2$ — a different physical model. Its claim
to "recover \eqref{eq:Ekin} at leading order" is therefore **false**.

$\rho_0$ does **not** shift: the main-line result is sound, the appendix is not.
