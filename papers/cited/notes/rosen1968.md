# rosen1968 — citation evidence with correction

G. Rosen, *Particlelike solutions to nonlinear complex scalar field theories
with positive-definite energy densities*, J. Math. Phys. **9**, 996–998
(1968).

Primary source page: DOI 10.1063/1.1664693 · pre-arXiv. The cited AIP article
is closed-access and no open full text was found.

## SSV usage

SSV-I now cites Rosen1968 only as a negative result: the paper explicitly says
that this 1968 article is not the source of the logarithmic field equation. It
identifies Rosen's 1969 paper as the relativistic precursor and attributes the
nonrelativistic LogSE used in SSV to BBM1976.

## Bibliographic check and correct source

The cited 1968 article is a three-page paper on finite-energy particlelike
solutions of nonlinear complex scalar field theories. The explicit
logarithmic relativistic field equation instead appears in Rosen's later
paper, *Dilatation covariance and exact solutions in local relativistic field
theories*, Phys. Rev. **183**, 1186–1188 (1969), DOI
10.1103/PhysRev.183.1186. Its Eq. (1) contains the self-interaction
\(g|\phi|^2\ln|\phi|^2\), and Eq. (2) contains the corresponding
\(g(1+\ln|\phi|^2)\phi\) field term.

The independently checked BBM1976 paper then introduces the nonrelativistic
logarithmic Schrödinger equation used in nonlinear wave mechanics.

**Verdict: `MISATTRIBUTED`.** Rosen belongs in the broader historical
lineage, but this specific article is not the source of the explicit
logarithmic field equation. SSV-I's present negative use matches this evidence;
BBM1976 remains the cited source for the nonrelativistic LogSE and Gausson.
