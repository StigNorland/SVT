## Source result and explanation

The abstract, printed p. 2333 (PDF p. 1), states that:

> the black-hole entropy is equal to the ratio of the black-hole area to the
> square of the Planck length times a dimensionless constant

It also formulates the generalized second law: ordinary exterior entropy plus
black-hole entropy never decreases. The 1973 paper thus establishes the
area-over-Planck-area form, but predates Hawking's temperature calculation
that fixes the conventional coefficient \(1/4\).

**Verdict: `OK`.** Bekenstein is the correct primary source for associating
black-hole entropy with horizon area in Planck units. The exact
Bekenstein–Hawking coefficient in SSV must rest on Hawking1975 as well, and
neither citation establishes SSV's microscopic horizon-cell interpretation
or its proposed identification \(\xi=l_P\).
