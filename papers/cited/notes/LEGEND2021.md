## Source result and explanation

The executive summary, p. 1, explains what a positive signal would establish:

> would prove unambiguously not only that new lepton-number-violating physics
> exists but also that it is connected to the mysterious origin of the
> neutrino’s mass.

The report calls `0νββ` the only known feasible probe of the neutrino's
Majorana nature. It then states that LEGEND-1000 is designed for a 99.7%-CL
discovery sensitivity beyond a `76Ge` half-life of `10^28 yr`, corresponding
to an effective Majorana-mass range of `9–21 meV` after ten years.

**Verdict: `OK`.** The primary report supports both components actually used
by SSV: LEGEND is designed to search for `0νββ`, and a confirmed discovery
would contradict a strictly Dirac, zero-Majorana-mass prediction.
