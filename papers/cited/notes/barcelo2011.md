## Theorem 1 (p.13), verbatim

> **Theorem 1** If a fluid is barotropic and inviscid, and the flow is
> irrotational (though possibly time dependent) then the equation of motion for
> the velocity potential describing a linearized acoustic disturbance φ₁ around
> some assumed background flow φ₀ is identical to the d'Alembertian equation of
> motion for a minimally-coupled massless scalar field propagating in a
> (3+1)-dimensional Lorentzian geometry [g₀]_ab specified by the background flow
>
> Δφ₁ ≡ (1/√−g₀) ∂_μ ( √−g₀ [g₀]^{μν} ∂_ν φ₁ ) = 0.   (6)
