## Source result and explanation

The abstract, printed p. 2, reports:

> The RMS deviations are less than 50 parts per million of the peak of the
> CMBR. For the Comptonization and chemical potential we find
> \(|y|<15\times10^{-6}\) and \(|\mu|<9\times10^{-5}\) (95% CL).

Section 6.2 obtains the chemical-potential limit after fitting a
Bose–Einstein spectral distortion, and Section 6.3 obtains the final
Comptonization limit after including Galactic-model systematics. Both are
95%-confidence upper limits.

**Verdict: `OK`.** The two numerical bounds and their use as observational
upper limits are exact. The paper constrains CMB spectral shapes; it does not
supply SSV's proposed map from defect statistics to distortion amplitudes,
which SSV appropriately labels uncomputed.
