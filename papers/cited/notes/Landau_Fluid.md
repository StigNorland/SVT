## SSV usage and independent equation check

SSV-II `main.tex:2475–2489` uses the compact-monopole pressure
\[
\delta p(r,t)={\rho_0\over4\pi r}\,
\ddot V(t-r/c).
\]
The standard open monopole result is
\[
p(r,t)={\rho_0\over4\pi r}{dQ(t-r/c)\over dt},
\qquad Q=\dot V,
\]
which is exactly SSV's equation. It independently verifies the formula and
its retarded-time and compact-source conditions.

**Verdict: `OK` by independent derivation, with primary-access limitation.**
The equation is correct standard acoustics and §74 is topically appropriate.
The citation does not support SSV's subsequently retired claim that a
time-averaged far-zone Bjerknes attraction remains inverse-square without
phase reversals.
