## Source result and explanation

The abstract identifies `0νββ` as a primary experimental probe of Majorana
neutrinos. In “Data Analysis and Results,” after Fig. 4 (PDF p. 5), the authors
report:

> The fit with the signal-plus-background model shows no evidence for 0νββ
> decay. We find the best fit at Γ0ν = (0.9 ± 1.4) × 10−26 yr−1

They set the corresponding 90%-credible lower limit

```tex
T_{1/2}^{0\nu}>2.2\times10^{25}\ {\rm yr}.
```

**Verdict: `OK`.** CUORE is correctly identified as an experiment capable of
testing the Majorana-neutrino channel used by SSV as a falsifier. The cited
paper reports no positive signal, so it does not itself falsify SSV.
