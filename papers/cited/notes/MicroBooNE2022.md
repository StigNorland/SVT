question:

> This article presents a measurement of \(\nu_e\) interactions without pions

It uses Fermilab's short-baseline Booster Neutrino Beam and tests whether
electron-neutrino interactions explain the MiniBooNE low-energy excess. The

the most sensitive channel consistent with no excess. It does not search
long-baseline or atmospheric data, and it does not test or establish a fourth
active flavour.
