## Source result and explanation

The paper's Eq. (3.3) selects the logarithmic equation with nonlinear term
\(-b\psi\ln(|\psi|^2a^3)\) and subsequently restricts to \(b>0\). Its
introduction, printed p. 63 (PDF p. 2), states:

> The nonlinear Schrodinger equation with logarithmic nonlinearity possesses
> solitonlike solutions of the gaussian shape (we call them gaussons) in any
> number of dimensions.

Equation (6.1), printed p. 77 (PDF p. 16), gives the freely moving normalized
Gaussian with exponent \(-(\mathbf r-\mathbf q-\mathbf vt)^2/(2\ell^2)\).
The paper defines
\(\ell=\hbar/\sqrt{2mb}\), so
\(\ell^2=\hbar^2/(2mb)\), and states that the packet obeys the logarithmic
wave equation. Printed p. 78 further says that in a uniform electric field
the Gausson follows classical motion while preserving exactly its Gaussian
shape.

**Verdict: `OK`.** The primary source supports the cited logarithmic
nonlinearity, Gaussian width, and exact shape preservation for its \(b>0\)
attractive sign convention. It does not support using that Gausson on SSV's
opposite, stable-vacuum branch. SSV-VII-a correctly marks that inherited
Gausson argument as non-current, and BBM cannot repair the branch mismatch.
