## SSV usage

## Source result and explanation

The conclusion, p. 32, gives the long-exposure phase sensitivity:

> For 15 years of exposure, δCP resolution between five and fifteen degrees
> are possible, depending on the true value of δCP.

Table 11 separately projects `10°` resolution at `δCP=0` after eight staged
years and `20°` at `δCP=-π/2` after twelve. These projections include
far-detector simulation, near-detector parameterization, and systematic
uncertainties.

**Verdict: `OK`.** The paper directly supports SSV's narrow statement that
DUNE can make a precision determination of the leptonic Dirac CP phase. It
does not support the proposed PMNS–CKM relation; SSV correctly says its own
relation awaits calculation.
