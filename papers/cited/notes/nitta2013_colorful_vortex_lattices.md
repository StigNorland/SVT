> constant and the rotation speed, we find triangular ordered "colorful" vortex

## Relevance

Corroborates `nitta2012_vortex_trimer`: the richer vortex structures that make a
three-way junction possible — fractional vortices each winding *one of three
components* — exist only because there are multiple components to wind. A
single-component U(1) condensate has one phase, hence only integer vortices and
no fractional or junction structure.

**Use verdict: `OK` as supporting evidence.** Consistent with the D2 no-go; adds
nothing SSV-I cites, and is not presented as a direct source for the no-go.
