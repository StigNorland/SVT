## Source result and explanation

The introduction, printed p. 8, describes the core result:

> Supersonic fluid flow can then generate a “dumb hole”, the acoustic analogue
> of a “black hole”, and the analogy can be extended all the way to
> mathematically demonstrating the presence of phononic Hawking radiation from
> the acoustic horizon.

Section 2 derives the acoustic metric, horizon, and surface gravity; Section
5.1 explains that Hawking emission is a kinematic quantum-field effect that
does not depend on Einstein's equations. The immediately following
introduction paragraph also cautions that analogy is not identity and does
not claim complete equivalence with general relativity.

**Verdict: `OK`.** The citation supports the mathematical acoustic metric and
Hawking-effect tools claimed. It cannot support SSV's further ontological
assertion that the analogue medium *is* the physical vacuum; SSV correctly
presents that as its own proposed difference, not as a result of the review.
