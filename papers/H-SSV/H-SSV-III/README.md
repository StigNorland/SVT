# H-SSV-III — Emergent Geometry and Lensing

**Status:** planned; depends on H-SSV-I and H-SSV-II.

## Scope

Derive the spacetime response of the screen theory:

- the temporal and spatial metric sectors from one screen state;
- universality and the equivalence principle;
- the weak-field PPN limit, especially $\gamma$;
- light deflection, Shapiro delay and gravitational redshift;
- wave propagation speed and polarisation content;
- preferred-frame and Lorentz-invariance constraints;
- the relationship to, and distinction from, an acoustic metric.

## Exit gate

The spatial response must be derived rather than copied from GR and must recover
the observed weak-field lensing strength, $\gamma\simeq1$, while retaining the
clock-rate mechanism. Local gravity, propagation and preferred-frame tests must
be stated as quantitative pass/fail conditions.

## Excluded from this paper

No claim that screen deformation automatically supplies lensing. The existing
bare-medium $\gamma\simeq0$ failure remains active until replaced by a derived
mechanism.
