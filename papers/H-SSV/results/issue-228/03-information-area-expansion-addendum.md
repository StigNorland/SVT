## R4 hypothesis

Persistent added screen records increase the effective screen area. Photon
wavepackets stretch because that physical screen geometry expands. This is a
metric response `B(t)`, not an energy-only tired-light channel. It may generate
spacetime curvature even when each spatial slice is intrinsically flat;
expansion and spatial curvature are therefore not assumed to be synonyms.

Let `N(t)` be the number of persistent distinguishable records occupying an
effective homogeneous screen, and let each occupied cell carry fixed area
`a_cell`. For an isotropic two-dimensional screen,

\[
\mathcal A_s=a_{\rm cell}N,
\qquad B\equiv\sqrt{\mathcal A_s/\mathcal A_{s0}}
=\sqrt{N/N_0}.
\]

The geometric redshift and transient stretch are then

\[
1+z={B_o\over B_e}=\sqrt{N_o/N_e},
\qquad {\Delta t_o\over\Delta t_e}=1+z.
\]

This is invariant and supplies the missing spatial response in form.

## Deceleration prediction

The screen expansion rate and acceleration are

\[
H_B={\dot B\over B}={1\over2}{\dot N\over N},
\qquad
{\ddot B\over B}={1\over2}{\ddot N\over N}
-{1\over4}\left({\dot N\over N}\right)^2.
\]

Hence genuine deceleration, `ddot B<0`, requires

\[
2N\ddot N<\dot N^2.
\]

For constant persistent-record production, `ddot N=0`, the deceleration
parameter is exactly `q_B=-B ddot B/dot B^2=1`. More generally, if
`N proportional t^p`, then `B proportional t^(p/2)` and `q_B=2/p-1`; the
screen decelerates for `0<p<2`.

This addendum interprets “decelerating” as `ddot B<0`. It is not the weaker
condition `dot H_B<0`, which can hold even during accelerated expansion.

## C4 ledger boundary

`N` cannot be the total global von Neumann entropy: C4's closed unitary ledger
keeps that information constant. It must be a reduced-state persistent-record
occupancy or activation of previously available screen sites. C4 does not yet
derive:

- the record-persistence/coarse-graining criterion;
- `a_cell` or the relation between occupied records and physical area;
- whether latent sites are activated or new Hilbert factors are created;
- the energy cost and stress associated with increasing area;
- `dot N`, `ddot N`, saturation, or a perturbation/growth law.

Without those items, `B=sqrt(N/N0)` is a transparent new constitutive postulate,
not a consequence of the C4 foundation.

## Observational consequence

If R4 is the sole redshift mechanism and `ddot B<0` at late times, it is a
metric decelerating cosmology and must face the standard candle/ruler evidence
directly. A calibration-independent, dynamics-independent SN analysis by
Seikel and Schwarz reports accelerated expansion under spatial flatness and
statistical homogeneity/isotropy ([arXiv:0810.4484](https://arxiv.org/abs/0810.4484)).
The assumptions and low-redshift sensitivity of that test must be retained.

R4 therefore yields a valuable falsifiable sign prediction, but currently
fails to derive its record law and is in tension with the registered late-time
distance evidence if its deceleration is imposed as the sole geometry. It does
not change the overall `UNDERDETERMINED` verdict: a mixed optical completion can
still hide geometry and wake in their product, while pure decelerating R4 is a
separately negative candidate.
