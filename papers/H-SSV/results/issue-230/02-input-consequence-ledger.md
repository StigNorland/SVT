## Not derived

- an objective coherence criterion or one-count screen-composition law;
- a mapping from baryonic structure to screen load;
- an independently fixed response amplitude or core radius;
- a true host/satellite susceptibility `eta`;
- quantum entanglement, a Hilbert-space factorization, or a no-signalling shared
  galaxy observable;
- a universal galaxy law or promotion of H-SSV-IV.

The observed UDG/SPARC contrast is therefore an input fact that remains
unexplained by the tested H-SSV proxy set.
