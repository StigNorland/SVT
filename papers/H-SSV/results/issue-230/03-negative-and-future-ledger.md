# Issue #230 — negative and future-data ledger

## Decisive negatives

1. **No common radial contract.** The six-UDG source supplies population summary
   velocities from two rings, while SPARC supplies reusable radial component
   curves. The one resolved UDG is graphical in the source archive. Model-family
   AICc values cannot be compared across those products.
2. **No excluded-class prediction.** `Gamma_dyn + fgas` improves ordinary LOO
   RMSE but worsens UDG-versus-SPARC class-holdout prediction relative to the
   intercept. It recognizes within-sample structure without predicting the
   matched contrast.
3. **No homogeneous turbulence/SFR proxy.** Four UDG gas dispersions are upper
   limits, comparable SPARC turbulence is absent, and individual UDG SFRs are
   not printed in the homogeneous table.
4. **No host test.** The selected gas-rich UDGs are fairly isolated and no true
   host membership, 3D separation, relative velocity, or orbital history is
   available. A shared latent would be invented rather than tested.
5. **No entanglement inference.** Cross-galaxy covariance was not established,
   much less a causal quantum construction.

## Conventional alternatives not eliminated

- inclination and intrinsic disc ellipticity, especially for AGC 114905;
- distance and baryonic mass-profile uncertainty;
- beam resolution and AGC 749290 oversampling;
- selection of unusually extended, gas-rich systems;
- non-axisymmetry, disequilibrium, or residual non-circular motion;
- ordinary tides, stripping, ram pressure, and backsplash for a future genuine
  satellite sample.

## Exact data that would reopen a stronger test

The next audit should not begin until it has:

- author-supplied per-ring `R, Vcirc, sigma_V, Vgas, Vstar` (or surface-density
  profiles sufficient to calculate them) for several UDGs, with covariance;
- independent distance and inclination likelihoods and drift-correction inputs;
- a prespecified external comparison set using the same reduction pipeline;
- for Gate C, a genuine satellite sample with host distance, systemic velocity,
  membership probability, and conventional environmental diagnostics; and
- at least one untouched galaxy or group class reserved before proxy selection.

DF2/DF4 still require a separate Jeans/tracer preregistration and cannot fill
this radial-data gap.
