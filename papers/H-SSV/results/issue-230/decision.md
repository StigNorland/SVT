# Issue #230 decision

**UNSUPPORTED — close as a completed negative exploration.**

The matched UDG/SPARC contrast is real at the published summary level, but the
tested internal proxies do not predict across the held-out class boundary. The
available publications do not provide a common reusable radial likelihood, so
no cross-class kernel or amplitude comparison is valid. The isolated discovery
sample also makes the host/shared-screen gate ineligible.

This decision does not reject the C4/C5 screen ontology. It says the current
data and provisional equations do not explain why a baryons-only-like slow UDG
differs from a mass-discrepant slow dwarf such as DDO154. H-SSV-IV remains
blocked; there is no universal screen law or entanglement claim.
