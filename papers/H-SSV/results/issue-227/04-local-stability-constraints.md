# Issue #227 — local, stability, and propagation constraints

## Static sector

T0 is an inverse-square conservative control with universal composition only
if every source couples through the same mass-energy charge. `G` is measured
input. The finite-domain cored-log and Gaussian representatives are bounded at
the origin and produce conservative central accelerations.

Using `a_*=1.2e-10 m s^-2` only as a conditional comparison gives a solar core
radius of about 7030 AU. For the cored-log representative, the direct screen
acceleration is about `2.88e-12` of Newtonian gravity at Earth and `2.53e-9` at
Saturn. These small direct fractions are not a Solar-System pass: the theory
does not predict how the Galactic shared screen produces an external tidal
field.

The frozen Cassini/DE440 comparison is `Q2=(1.6 +/- 1.8)e-27 s^-2`. T3 supplies
no `Q2`, so it cannot be scored against that measurement.

## Dynamic sector

A static conservative potential does not establish bounded memory or negligible
secular drag under moving sources. C4/T3 currently supplies none of:

- a retarded response action or Green function;
- binary radiation power or orbital-decay prediction;
- propagating polarization content;
- a rule for releasing and reallocating saturated patch records;
- equality of the gravitational propagation speed with `c`.

GW170817/GRB 170817A constrains the fractional speed difference to roughly
`[-3e-15, 7e-16]`. The C4 causal statement `c_screen<=c` is much weaker and is
not a pass. G4 and G6 therefore fail pending a dynamical theory.
