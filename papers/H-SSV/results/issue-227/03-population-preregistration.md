# Conditional future population prediction

Status: **recorded but not activated**

If a future microscopic derivation fixes one universal `a_*`, selects a unique
positive regulator, and closes G4/G6, the preregistered population mapping is

\[
r_c(M_{\rm bar})=\sqrt{G M_{\rm bar}/a_*},\qquad
V_\infty(M_{\rm bar})=(G M_{\rm bar}a_*)^{1/4}.
\]

Before opening any galaxy sample, that future test must freeze:

- the baryonic-mass estimator and distance/inclination treatment;
- the single global value and independent provenance of `a_*`;
- the core kernel and outer-boundary prescription;
- the mapping from observed extended baryons, rather than point mass, to the
  compact screen patch;
- the host/satellite shared-patch rule and isolation criterion;
- scatter and failure thresholds without per-galaxy screen parameters.

The primary population prediction is slope four in `M_bar` versus flat
velocity. A secondary prediction is `r_c proportional sqrt(M_bar)`. Neither is
claimed observationally confirmed here. H-SSV-IV remains blocked because the
amplitude, regulator, and dynamics are not independently frozen.
