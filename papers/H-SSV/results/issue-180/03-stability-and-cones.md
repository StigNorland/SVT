## Principal cone versus infrared sound cone

The canonical covariant scalar has metric principal symbol

\[
g^{\mu\nu}k_\mu k_\nu,
\]

so its high-frequency characteristics share the metric light cone. The
finite-density rotating background mixes amplitude and phase, producing the
infrared Goldstone cone in equation (7). These are different questions.

For R0 the infrared cone is not merely subluminal: it is imaginary. For the
stable sign-reversed control it is real but strictly inside the metric cone.
Consequently the control does not realize the old SSV identification
“Goldstone propagation speed \(=c\)” in a controlled nonrelativistic regime.

Adding canonical Maxwell, Dirac, and Einstein kinetic terms would make their
principal symbols share the metric cone, but it cannot change the scalar
eigenvalue continuously without changing the scalar action at leading
quadratic order.

## Ghost and tachyon classification

- The canonical time-derivative matrix has the healthy sign: there is no
  Ostrogradsky or wrong-sign kinetic ghost in R0.
- The failure is a gradient/modulational instability of the Goldstone branch.
- The interaction energy is additionally unbounded below for \(B>0\).
- At \(B\ge m\), the \(k=0\) massive branch also becomes non-positive.

Thus “canonical kinetic term” is not sufficient for a stable condensate.

## Strong-coupling question

No strong-coupling scale needs to be fitted to decide P3. The instability is
present at arbitrarily small nonzero \(k\), inside any proposed
long-wavelength EFT domain. Lowering the ultraviolet cutoff cannot remove it.
For the same reason, the SSV clock interpretation cannot be recovered as a
stable propagating low-energy mode about this background: the would-be clock
phase has exponentially growing long-wavelength perturbations.
