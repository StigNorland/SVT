## Independent audit conclusions

1. The decisive K3 result does not rely on Weinberg–Witten,
   Nielsen–Ninomiya, or an observational bound. It follows from the frozen
   scalar equation's own quadratic spectrum.
2. No theorem in the table says that an ordinary Einstein + gauge + chiral
   EFT is impossible. That control is familiar and anomaly-free.
3. The obstruction is the conjunction demanded by #180: **that ordinary
   healthy structure must coexist with, and retain, the literal SSV
   condensate limit**.
4. Holography can change which assumptions of some no-go theorems apply, but
   it cannot turn a negative eigenvalue of the claimed low-energy bulk limit
   into a positive one without changing that limit.
