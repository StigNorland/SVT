# H-SSV-V — Screen Cosmology

**Status:** planned; separate from the galactic-gravity derivation.

## Scope

Develop the global-screen branch:

- homogeneous global loading and the absence of transverse refraction;
- the invariant physical structure beyond a removable homogeneous lapse;
- a derived spatial-scale or photon--screen propagation law;
- the decomposition of geometric, wake, peculiar and local gravitational
  redshifts;
- a capacity scale from deep-space energy density with a derived information
  cost per write;
- cosmic distance, lookback time, age, horizon and size relations;
- transient time stretching, surface brightness, distance duality, background
  radiation, primordial abundances, structure growth, standard rulers and
  redshift drift.

## Exit gate

One fixed global evolution law must jointly reproduce redshift, time stretching
and independent distance/ruler/clock observations. The result must not rely on
assigning the linear redshift term to wake and the nonlinear term to expansion;
both contributions must be derived and fitted jointly.

## Excluded from this paper

No distance inferred from quasar redshift may be reused as an independent test
of the same redshift law. No claim about an older, younger, larger, smaller,
static or eternal universe precedes the derived distance and clock relations.
