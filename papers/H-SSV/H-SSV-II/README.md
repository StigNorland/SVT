# H-SSV-II — Screen-Update Gravity

**Status:** planned; depends on H-SSV-I.

## Scope

Derive gravity from the frozen screen foundation:

- screen loading to update availability $A=d\tau/dt$;
- $\Phi=c^2\ln A$ and $\mathbf g=-c^2\nabla\ln A$;
- sign-definite attraction and source superposition;
- Newtonian and Solar-System limits;
- the static two-dimensional Green function and its bulk mapping;
- the regularised cored-logarithmic potential and core scale;
- the amplitude law, including the baryonic Tully--Fisher scaling target;
- memory regulation and the absence of excessive secular orbital drag;
- a complete energy ledger.

## Exit gate

The theory must derive its potential shape, amplitude and transition/core scale
from H-SSV-I quantities without galaxy-by-galaxy fitting. It must recover the
known local weak-field limit and make preregisterable galaxy-scale predictions.

## Excluded from this paper

No relabelling of the cored-logarithmic law as a standard pseudo-isothermal
halo, and no restoration of the falsified coherent pressure-wave source.
