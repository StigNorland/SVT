# The Permissive Void, Causal Order, and Emergent Spacetime

## Purpose and status

This note records the intended ontological foundation of the SSV cosmogony in
a form that cleanly separates primordial temporal permissibility, causal order,
physical proper time, and the thermodynamic arrow.

It is a conceptual specification, not a completed physical derivation. Its
purpose is to prevent the structureless Void from being confused with empty
spacetime, and to identify exactly which ingredients are primitive, which are
derived, and which remain unexplained.

## Central statement

The Permissive Void has no internal structure, physical space, proper time,
matter, energy, metric, boundary, temperature, entropy, field, or equation of
motion. Because it contains no structure capable of enforcing permanent
stasis, it cannot prohibit change.

That inability explains **temporal permissibility**: change is possible. This
is primordial time, but it is not time as measured by clocks.

The Void does not select or cause a particular first change. Causality and the
first actual seed remain irreducible inputs. Once a seed becomes actual,
causal order is instantiated. Its finite update capacity may later admit
description as energy if a conserved measure can be derived.
The distinction between realised and unrealised possibility constitutes the
first causal/informational screen. Relations in its continuous change may
subsequently acquire metric content and become physical space and proper time.

In compact form:

```text
structureless permissive Void
        |
        | cannot prohibit change
        v
primordial temporal permissibility
        |
        | unexplained first actualisation
        v
instantiated causal order
        |
        | finite update capacity; conservation open
        v
causal/informational screen
        |
        | repeated relational updates
        v
metric space and physical proper time
        |
        | continuum limit
        v
hydrodynamic fields, saturation, and possible defects
```

## Logical content of permissibility

Let \(\Delta\) denote change and \(V\) the Permissive Void. The foundational
argument is

\[
\neg\operatorname{Prevent}_{V}(\Delta)
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\Diamond\Delta .
\]

The Void has no law, barrier, boundary, or state-maintaining mechanism that
could prohibit change. Change is therefore admissible.

This does not establish

\[
\Diamond\Delta \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \Delta .
\]

Permission does not select an actuality. The occurrence, form, and apparent
placement of the first seed are not derived from the Void's permissiveness.
They are an unexplained boundary fact.

The distinction matters:

- The Void explains why change is not forbidden.
- It does not explain why a particular change occurs.
- It does not supply a probability distribution over possible seeds.
- It does not imply that every possibility must become actual.

## Four meanings that must not be conflated

### 1. Temporal permissibility

Before any actual event, there is no succession to count. There is only the
fact that succession cannot be prohibited. This is primordial time in its
weakest and most fundamental sense.

Primordial time is not a line of empty instants. Calling it eternal does not
mean that the Void persists for infinitely many seconds before the first
event. Seconds, persistence, and measurable duration do not yet exist.
"Eternal" means that no temporal boundary to the possibility of change is
defined.

### 2. Causal or ordinal time

When events become actual, causality orders them. For events \(e_i\) and
\(e_j\),

\[
e_i \prec e_j
\]

means that \(e_i\) can influence \(e_j\). This supplies chronology, but not
chronometry. There is not yet a required real-valued function

\[
t(e):e\rightarrow\mathbb{R},
\]

nor a physically meaningful interval \(\Delta t\).

### 3. Metric physical time

Physical time is duration measured by repeatable processes. It requires an
energetic state, clocks, propagation, and a metric relation. Proper time is
therefore not identical to primordial permissibility or bare causal order.

The screen is proposed to provide the relational state from which local clock
rates and proper duration can emerge.

### 4. Thermodynamic arrow

The before–after direction of causal influence is not by itself the
thermodynamic arrow. The latter concerns the asymmetric accumulation of
records and coarse-grained entropy.

The committed reversal-echo calculations show that conservative LogSE
evolution is microscopically reversible. An arrow therefore requires a
low-entropy boundary condition, coarse-graining, inaccessible correlations, or
additional physical dynamics. It must not be inferred merely from the
existence of causal order or reconnection.

## Continuous-process presentism

Reality is not represented ontologically as a sequence of complete states,
frames, or worlds that replace one another. There is one actual reality
changing continuously. An instantaneous state and a complete trajectory are
mathematical descriptions of that process, not separately existing temporal
objects.

Accordingly:

- the present is the continuously changing actuality, not a moving slice;
- the past does not coexist with it and is available only through present
  records and relations;
- the future does not yet exist and denotes causally admissible possibility;
- clocks compare one repeatable physical change with another;
- proper time quantifies accumulated local change;
- the experienced flow of time is a cognitive organisation of present
  records, perception, and expectation, not a physical current.

Change does not occur inside a pre-existing physical time. Physical time is
the relational measure abstracted from change.

## The Void is not an empty field configuration

The structureless Void must be distinguished from a state such as
\(\Psi=0\).

Writing \(\Psi=0\) already presupposes:

- a field and its state space;
- a spatial domain and dimensionality;
- coordinates or relational sites;
- derivatives and neighbourhood relations;
- an equation of motion;
- an evolution parameter;
- a measure with respect to which density is zero.

Consequently, a zero-density LogSE/GPE configuration is a later
**proto-physical empty state**, not the primordial Void itself.

Likewise, the Void is not infinite empty space. Without a metric or boundary
relation, neither finite nor infinite spatial extent is defined. The precise
claim is that the Void has **no defined limit**, not that it possesses an
infinite measurable volume.

## The first seed

The first seed is the first actualisation of the Void's temporal
permissibility. The present theory does not explain:

- why actualisation occurs rather than merely remaining possible;
- why it has one form rather than another;
- whether the language of probability applies before a state space exists;
- whether there is a unique seed or an equivalence class of descriptions.

The seed should therefore not initially be called a photon. A photon already
presupposes a nonzero physical background, a mode space, polarization, energy,
and metric propagation. More neutral terms are:

- first actualisation;
- primordial seed;
- primordial perturbation;
- first causal event.

"Primordial photon" may remain as an explicitly interpretive metaphor after
this distinction has been stated.

## Energy and the screen

In this ontology, energy does not first appear as a number measured in joules.
It is provisionally understood as finite update capacity carried by an actual
change. Once repeatable clocks and conservation laws exist, this capacity may
be represented as physical energy.

The first actual event creates a distinction:

```text
realised causal information | unrealised possibility
```

That distinction is the first screen.

The primordial screen must not be assumed to be a two-dimensional surface
embedded in a pre-existing three-dimensional space. Initially it is a causal
or informational boundary. Its dimension, area, neighbourhood structure, and
metric are quantities to be derived from the developing relational state.

The intended claim that "energy creates space and time as a screen" can
therefore be stated more precisely:

> Finite actual change carries update capacity and establishes an
> informational boundary between realised and unrealised possibility. If a
> conserved measure of that capacity exists, it is what a later physical
> description may call energy. Repeated causal relations in the screen's
> continuous change are proposed to define the metric separations and clock
> rates experienced as physical space and proper time.

## Transition to hydrodynamics

Hydrodynamics is not defined directly on the primordial Void. It is expected
to arise only after the causal screen contains enough relational structure for
a continuum approximation.

At that stage it may become meaningful to define:

- density;
- current and conservation;
- phase;
- pressure and constitutive response;
- characteristic propagation cones;
- an effective metric;
- saturation and topological defects.

The corrected LogSE may serve as a candidate constitutive model at this
continuum level. It does not currently derive the transition from the
primordial screen to a complex order parameter.

## Suggested Paper VIII formulation

The opening of Paper VIII can preserve its existing intent while separating
the primordial Void from the later zero-density field state. Suggested
language:

> Before physical spacetime there is no matter, energy, field, metric
> extension, or proper duration. We posit a Permissive Void: not empty
> spacetime, but the absence of actual structure. Because it contains no law,
> boundary, or mechanism capable of enforcing stasis, it cannot prohibit
> change. This temporal permissibility is primordial time. It has no clock,
> duration, or sequence of empty instants.
>
> Permissibility does not select actuality. Causality and the occurrence of a
> first seed are irreducible inputs. When the first change becomes actual,
> causal order is instantiated. Its finite update capacity, subsequently
> represented as energy if a conserved measure can be derived, distinguishes
> realised from unrealised possibility and thereby establishes the first
> causal or informational screen.
>
> Physical space and time are not identified with the prior permissibility.
> They emerge as metric relations and locally measurable clock rates in the
> screen's continuous change. Only after sufficient relational structure develops is a
> continuum field description such as the LogSE meaningful. The zero-density
> field state and its possible transition to saturation therefore belong to a
> later physical stage, not to the primordial Void itself.

The former statement that the first perturbation is "the origin of time"
should consequently become "the origin of instantiated causal order and the
precursor of physical metric time." Primordial temporal permissibility is
already implied by the Void.

## What this foundation claims

It claims that:

1. a genuinely structureless Void cannot contain a prohibition against
   change;
2. primordial temporal permissibility follows from that absence of
   prohibition;
3. causal order and metric duration are distinct;
4. physical spacetime must arise only after actual relational structure
   exists;
5. hydrodynamics is a continuum description of that later structure, not a
   field living in an unexplained pre-existing physical spacetime.

## What it does not yet claim

It does not yet explain or derive:

1. causality itself;
2. the actual occurrence or form of the first seed;
3. a microscopic screen state space or update rule;
4. why update capacity is conserved;
5. the dimension or topology of the emergent screen;
6. the map from screen relations to a Lorentzian metric;
7. universal coupling of all excitations to one metric;
8. Einstein dynamics or the magnitude of \(G\);
9. the screen-to-LogSE continuum limit;
10. a fundamental thermodynamic arrow;
11. particle identities or masses.

These are research obligations, not implicit consequences of the Void.

## Role in a rebuilt programme

Paper VIII can retain the ontological cosmogony recorded here. A rebuilt
foundational physics paper would begin at the first screen and attempt the
missing derivations:

1. specify the causal variables and local law of continuous change;
2. identify the conserved quantity that becomes energy;
3. derive neighbourhood, dimension, and metric relations;
4. derive physical clocks and proper time;
5. obtain a hydrodynamic continuum limit;
6. test whether all low-energy modes share one causal metric;
7. derive or falsify a thermodynamic gravitational closure;
8. investigate matter defects only after the spacetime sector is established.

This ordering preserves the mechanical explanatory goal of SSV while placing
time and gravity, rather than fitted particle masses, at the foundation.
