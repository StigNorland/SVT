# SSV programme claim status

Current through [issue #220](https://github.com/StigNorland/SVT/issues/220),
following the Paper I normalization correction in
[issue #218](https://github.com/StigNorland/SVT/issues/218).

The programme is **closed as a route to deriving the observed particle,
gravity, and cosmological magnitudes**. “Genuinely open” below means that a
mathematical or physical question remains unanswered; it does not restore the
closed programme or count as evidence for SSV.

## Genuinely open

These questions have not been answered by the committed calculations.

| question | what would decide it | current boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Does the corrected 3D LogSE support stable particle-like defects with useful spectra? | converged 3D corrected-action solutions plus coupled BdG stability, without target fitting | #218 only reran saved-state calibrations and focused reduced operators |
| What is the corrected coupled-BdG amplitude spectrum? | basis- and box-converged coupled \(L/M\) calculation | the translation Goldstone control passes; higher branches remain diagnostic |
| What sets a reconnection cap and its coefficient? | nonlinear 3D dynamics with scale separation, deriving rather than inserting the cap radius | desktop harnesses are box-contaminated; no cap coefficient is derived |
| Does colour/weak chirality locking occur? | derive the cross-coupling sign and test coincident cores under nonlinear relaxation | the corrected radial overlap is nonzero but sign and geometry are uncomputed |
| Can a single corrected model produce a screen-to-bulk map? | one state space with modular first law, entropy map, stress/metric dictionary, and a kernel derived from screen data | [#166 audit](../papers/SSV-VII-b/results/reconstruction-assumption-audit-issue166.md) finds every load-bearing bridge missing |
| What selects the low-entropy past? | a boundary-condition or dynamical account beyond reversible LogSE evolution | the committed work supplies only coarse-graining |
| Is the cosmological \(a_0\) coefficient selected? | discriminating high-redshift evolution and a derived absolute cosmological scale | present data do not select \(1/(2\pi)\) over nearby alternatives |

## Bottom line

What survives is mostly model mathematics, known analogue-gravity form, and
sharp negative results. What fails is the programme’s original promise to
derive observed magnitudes. What remains open is scientifically legitimate but
does not count as a surviving prediction until it passes a preregistered,
target-independent calculation.
